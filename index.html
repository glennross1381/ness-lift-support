Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my workout and nutrition data stored?
    100% locally on your device. NESS Lift is built with an offline-first vault architecture so you never lose your logs, even with zero gym reception.
  • How do I back up or export my data?
    Open the Vault menu inside the app to export or restore your full JSON backup, or export your nutrition logs to CSV from the Nutrition tab.
  • Does NESS Lift require a subscription or account?
    No. There is no account creation, no cloud lock-in, and no tracking.
  • How do I use the Apple Watch app?
    Install the NESS Lift Watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone to access your Lift tracker, Rest timer, Macro log, and Plate calculator directly on your wrist.
