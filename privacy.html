NESS Lift Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

NESS Lift ("the App") is designed as a 100% offline-first personal workout and nutrition tracker. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal information, workout logs, nutrition records, body weight entries, or analytics data.

2. On-Device Storage

All workout splits, logged sets, baselines, nutrition entries, body weight logs, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. You have full control to export or delete your data at any time from within the App.

3. Apple Health & Biometrics

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration or Face ID / Touch ID vault lock, permissions are handled exclusively by iOS and watchOS on your device. HealthKit data and biometric authentication data never leave your device and are never transmitted to any external server or third party.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

NESS Lift contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no user profiling. If you tap an external exercise video link in the Form Guide, your device opens that link in your browser subject to that website's own privacy policy.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact glennros1381@gmail.com or visit our Support Page.
